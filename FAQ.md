## Q2: "Does libpath have its own unit-tests?"

Yes. Automated **unit** tests live under **test/unit**; they are expected to
**pass** (see **run_all_unit_tests.sh** in [INSTALL.md](./INSTALL.md)).

**Scratch** programs under **test/scratch** are for human inspection and
smoke (for example **test.scratch.versions**); they are not the automated
unit suite.
